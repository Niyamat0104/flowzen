💡 FlowZen Interactive Feature Tour

Welcome to FlowZen!

FlowZen combines real-time collaborative Kanban boards with an intelligent workflow engine. Here is how to get the most out of your workspace:

1. 🎯 Dynamic Task Risk Prediction (0–100)

Every active task is evaluated using deadline pressure, priority, estimated hours, and dependencies. Risks are categorized into Low, Moderate, High, and Critical with explainable text insights.

2. 🤖 "What Should I Work On Next?" Recommendation

FlowZen ranks active tasks using weighted scoring (30% Urgency, 25% Risk, 20% Priority, 15% Dependencies, 10% Effort) to surface your highest-value focus task.

3. ⏱️ Predicted Effort & Category Ratios

Whenever tasks move to Done, FlowZen learns your actual execution speed per category (Backend, Frontend, Design, Docs) to predict realistic effort.

4. ⚡ Real-Time Multi-Tab Broadcast Sync

Open multiple windows or browser tabs side-by-side. Card edits and drag-and-drop moves synchronize instantly (<50ms) across windows!
